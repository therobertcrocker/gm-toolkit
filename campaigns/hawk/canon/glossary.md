# H.A.W.K Glossary

H.A.W.K terms, one entry per term.

## Aether — `aether`

The raw magical energy that fills the Astral Sea and powers its vessels and technology.

It must be processed and concentrated into stable forms, Aetherstones and Aether-Cores, before it can be used. Older texts call it raw magical essence, "the possibility of anything that can be made real".

## Aether-Core — `aether-core`

A large, stable store of concentrated Aether; the big version of an Aetherstone.

Arcane casters off the Material Plane draw on Aether-Cores when the Weave cannot reach them.

## Aethership — `aethership`

A vessel powered by Aether, able to cross the Astral Sea for exploration, trade or combat.

## Aetherstone — `aetherstone`

A small, stable store of concentrated Aether, used to fuel Aether-tech.

## Aether-tech — `aether-tech`

Devices and infrastructure powered by Aether, from Aetherships to arcane machinery.

Every piece of Aether-tech runs on an Aetherstone or an Aether-Core, so it works without the Weave.

## Arcane Intelligence — `arcane-intelligence`

A magical artificial mind.

## Arcano-Tech — `arcano-tech`

Magical technology that began on the Material Plane and needs a constant link to the Weave to work.

Before 655 it ran on raw Threads of Magic, and it was clunky and unstable. The Weave made it reliable, which is why Arcano-Tech boomed after the Year of Dark.

## Astral God — `astral-god`

A powerful, god-like being that rules a sector of the Astral Sea and shapes its magic and its politics.

It is a manifestation of the pure, concentrated Aether of its domain, and it feeds on the life force, belief and power of the beings within it. Older texts call the Astral Gods Aetheric Divinities, the "true" gods.

## Astral Realms — `astral-realms`

The places out in the Astral Sea, as seen from the Material Plane.

## Astral Sea — `astral-sea`

The vast magical expanse between realms, home to the sectors and the Astral Gods.

## Axis — `axis`

One of the three pairs of opposed traits that Aether carries: Light and Dark, Order and Chaos, Activity and Passivity.

Older texts call the traits Alignments. Each Divine Realm leans toward one end of one axis. The Material Plane holds all six traits in balance.

## Contract — `contract`

The bargain behind occult and primal magic: a pact with a patron, sealed by sacrifice or ceremony.

The magic is bound to the contract's terms and ends when the contract is complete. The Fey Royals provide the contracts, so beyond the Material Plane this magic works only near the influence of a Fey Royal.

## Core Sector — `core-sector`

The central and most developed sector of the Astral Sea, fiercely contested by the major factions.

It is neutral ground. Blackwater used to control it.

## Diem Veritas — `diem-veritas`

"The Day of Truth", 29 Peony 655, when every being learned the truth about the gods.

It is now a Material Plane holiday, when communities air the truths and secrets they learned that year.

## Divine Realm — `divine-realm`

A sector aligned toward one end of one axis, and the realm of the Astral God it holds.

Each of the six rim sectors is a Divine Realm.

## Dragonkin — `dragonkin`

The people of dragon descent.

A Dragonkin colony in the Thymian ruins broke away from the Queendom of Dragons in 660.

## The Drift — `drift`

The space between realms in the Astral Sea, full of unpredictable magical phenomena and dangerous entities.

Aetherships ride its currents; the navigator's skill and the ship's Aether engines decide whether a passage is safe. On the Core Sector grid, the clouds show the Drift.

## Eon Arcanum — `eon-arcanum`

The Era of Magic, the current era, written EA.

## Eon Deus — `eon-deus`

The Divine Era before the Era of Magic, written ED. It began when the Aetheric Gods took over from the Fey Royals.

## Era of Strife — `era-of-strife`

The Great War, a conflict of about three hundred years from 140 EA that formed today's nations.

## Fey Royals — `fey-royals`

The powerful fey rulers of the Material Plane, its "old gods".

They were the gods mortals worshipped in the First Era. The Aetheric Gods took over from them, which began the Divine Era. The Weave strengthened their return. The Pact bound them until Luna broke it; since then they have reached beyond the Feywild and the Shadowfell, even into the Astral Sea.

## Feywild — `feywild`

One of the two realms the Fey Royals rule, with the Shadowfell.

The Pact confined the Fey Royals to the Feywild and the Shadowfell until it broke.

## The Forged — `forged`

Sentient mechanical people. Older texts call them Warforged.

## Fragment — `fragment`

A world in the Astral Sea: a piece of concentrated Aether that has taken form as a habitable or otherwise important place.

The word covers planet, world and realm. It is called a fragment because it is a fragment of the Astral Sea itself, holding Aether from only a few traits. Older texts say Astral Fragment.

## Gate — `gate`

A passage between sectors of the Astral Sea, used by Aetherships.

The charted gates out of the Core Sector are Solar Gate, Axial Gate, Tranquil Gate and Abaddon's Gate.

## Immaterial — `immaterial`

What lies beyond the Material, home to the entities Warlocks draw power from.

Old records speak of "Immaterial Storms" in the age before mortals.

## Material Plane — `material-plane`

The largest and most familiar plane, where most mortal civilizations live; also called Myr, or the Material.

It is the same kind of thing as a Fragment, but it is the one perfect nexus of all six traits. It is distinct from the Astral Sea, and Veil is the gateway between them.

## Metal/Chroma Hybrid — `metal-chroma-hybrid`

Dragonkin of mixed lineage. Many emigrated to the Dragonkin colony after 660.

## Myr — `myr`

The Material Plane's own name.

## The Pact — `pact`

The binding that held the Fey Royals and stopped at least some of them from acting directly.

It was formed at the end of the Divine Era. It was never meant to allow arcane magic; that it opened the way to the Era of Magic was a happy accident. It broke the moment Luna's Gate ignited in the finale of *The Coming War*, and it stays broken even if Luna is defeated.

## Pantheon Wars — `pantheon-wars`

The wars of the Divine Era, when theocratic empires fought the gods' proxy wars and destroyed themselves.

## Peony — `peony`

A month of the year.

## Phoenix-Class Adventurer — `phoenix-class-adventurer`

A class of adventurers sanctioned by H.A.W.K. The party was the first group to earn it, in 705.

## Questing as a Service — `questing-as-a-service`

H.A.W.K's name for its business: adventuring help for parties that pass its sanctioning. Written QaaS.

## Sanctioning — `sanctioning`

H.A.W.K's vetting process for adventuring parties.

## Sector — `sector`

A large, distinct region of the Astral Sea, with its own character, resources and power dynamics.

The Core Sector sits at the centre, with six rim sectors around it.

## Shadowfell — `shadowfell`

One of the two realms the Fey Royals rule, with the Feywild.

The Pact confined the Fey Royals to the Feywild and the Shadowfell until it broke.

## Soul Bond — `soul-bond`

The bond behind divine magic: a believer's soul bound to a god and its realm.

It gives raw access to the realm's power anywhere in the Astral Sea, but the god slowly devours the soul. That fact was kept hidden for a long time.

## Thread of Magic — `thread-of-magic`

A tiny hole in reality that the earliest arcane casters pulled raw, unstable power through. Precursor to The Weave.

## The 'Verse — `verse`

The whole cosmos.

## Wayfinder — `wayfinder`

H.A.W.K's assistant for a sanctioned party: a handler linked to the Weave that tracks factions and their attitude to the party.

Its link may falter off the Material Plane.

## The Weave — `weave`

The structured network of magical currents that allows controlled spellcasting and the shaping of magical effects.

More reliable than raw Aether, but less abundant. Morgana built it during her exile from the Thymian Court and released it to the Material Plane in 655; it exists only on the Material Plane, and only mortals control it. O.W.L. and other mortal factions are working to extend it.

## Wizard Advisor — `wizard-advisor`

A wizard trained to keep theoretical magical knowledge alive as society shifts to Arcano-Tech.

## Year of Ash — `year-of-ash`

The year planar energy mutated most of the Huron region's people, in 410 or 455 EA.

## Year of Dark — `year-of-dark`

655, when the Thymian Court eclipsed the moon and arcane magic stopped working.

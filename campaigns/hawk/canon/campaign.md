# H.A.W.K Campaign

The campaign, the story so far, and what the faction game does in it.

## The Campaign

The campaign runs in three seasons:

1. **The Fall of Magic** — set in 655, the Year of Dark. A different party, played by mostly the same players, stops a plot to remove arcane magic from the world. Of its characters, only Kas carries over to the current party. Their success sets off an explosion of magical infrastructure and the wide adoption of arcano-tech.
2. **The Coming War** — the current season, now in its final arc. The party opposes Blackwater Industries and deals with the rising tension between the Astral Gods as Blackwater's control weakens.
3. **The War in Heaven** — the third and final season, and the one the faction game serves. It deals with the aftermath of season two and the cataclysmic war between the Astral Gods.

The party is a crew of independent operators working against the corporations that control Aether, Blackwater Industries above all. With their crew aboard the Aethership *The Audacity*, they navigate the political and corporate intrigue of the Astral Sea, chase their own goals, and uncover the deeper schemes of the Astral Gods.

In *The War in Heaven*, the party faces the consequences of the earlier seasons: the war between the Astral Gods and the shifting balance of power in the Astral Sea. Their choices decide the fate of both the mortal and the divine realms.

## The Story So Far

The story stands at the finale arc of *The Coming War*. The arc is planned but not yet played, so its outcome is TBD.

### The Choice

At the end of the season, three crises are set in front of the party, and they can chase only one:

- **Luna's Gate** — Luna, a Fey Royal, moves to break the Pact and open an infernal portal into the Material Plane.
- **The Nest** — Lady Witherblight carries the M.T. Nest Device toward Axiom to destroy Blackwater Industries.
- **The Coup** — Seraphina Alderleaf makes a bid to unseat Ace Alchemical and take the Charm.

The party chooses to stop Luna. The other two crises play out without them. Witherblight's run resolves off-screen. Seraphina's coup breaks out in the middle of the party's fight, so they can influence it, but only indirectly and never for free.

### The Arc

**The Call.** Nyx, a Fey Royal of justice, cannot act against Luna herself because the Pact binds her. She reaches the party through Arabesque, her instrument, and tells them Luna is finally making her move. Luna is not summoning something that will pass. She is building a door to the Abyss that is meant to stay open forever, and she is building it in Porta Sol, Ace Alchemical's company city.

**The Hunt.** The party arrives in a city holding its breath. Seraphina is quietly gathering allies for her coup and reaches out to them. Luna has set her ritual but not yet lit it. In this short calm the party scouts and investigates, and the arc gets its quieter character moments before everything breaks open.

**The Marathon.** The coup breaks out into civil war inside the city walls, and its violence ignites Luna's Gate. For a day and a half the city tears itself apart. Ace and Seraph Industries fight district by district, the civilians caught between them pay for it, and demons start leaking into the streets. The party races to break the ritual's anchors. Each anchor is bound to one of Lilith's demon children and fed by some ugly habit of the city. Seraphina keeps asking for help, and the party learns the hard way that helping her makes the Gate stronger.

**The Gate Comes Apart.** The party breaks enough anchors and the Gate begins to collapse. Luna is exposed at its heart, and they move on her.

**The Flip.** Cornered, Luna would rather become something else than lose. She gives herself to Lilith and becomes her vessel. The final battle takes place on the collapsing Gate, against what Luna has turned into.

**The Aftermath.** The party counts the cost: who holds Porta Sol now, how the coup ended, and what became of Seraphina, the Charm and Luna. Off-screen, Lady Witherblight reaches Axiom with the M.T. Nest Device, and something unexpected rides along with it.

### How It Can End

- **Defeat** — the party cannot break the Gate in time, or Luna-as-Lilith beats them. The door opens for good. Demons and gods pour into the Material Plane in a mass-casualty event, and Asmodeus invades. The war begins on the worst possible terms.
- **Victory at a cost** — the party breaks just enough of the ritual to get through. The city survives, but the party reaches Luna worn down and the final fight is harder.
- **Victory, pushing their luck** — the party stays longer and breaks every anchor while the city burns around them. Luna is weaker when they finally reach her.
- **Seraphina's cavalry** — if the party backed the coup enough for it to succeed, despite the cost, Seraphina arrives at the turning point of the final battle. The help that hurt them earlier becomes what saves them.
- **Alone** — if the coup failed or the party left Seraphina to her own fight, they face the vessel without her.

### Still Open

- **Luna's fate** — does she die, get consumed, or survive in some form?
- **Arabesque** — her storyline with Nyx and the Endless she hosts is improvised at the table.
- **The final note** — the season ends cleanly with a seed for what comes next, or on a cliffhanger.

> **GM-only:** Asmodeus engineered all of it. Lilith's chaos is only his cover, and the players are meant to believe the threat is Lilith for as long as possible. The tell is plotting too precise to come from pure chaos, and the arc ends on it: after the aftermath comes the realization that the ritual was too precise to be the work of chaos. When the whole truth lands is still open: at the climax, in the aftermath, or held for the next season.

### Where Season 3 Opens

- Blackwater Industries has fallen as the overlord megacorporation. Splinter factions that carry its name fight on in *The War in Heaven*.
- The Core Sector is neutral ground. Blackwater used to control it.
- Ulsidor Blackwater is alive, but takes no part in season 3.
- The Pact broke the moment Luna's Gate ignited, and it stays broken even if Luna is defeated. The Fey Royals are free to reach beyond the Feywild and the Shadowfell.

## The Faction Game

The faction game tracks the shifting power between factions during *The War in Heaven*. It gives a structured view of who is gaining or losing influence, and offers plot hooks for the party to engage with those changes.

- **Start** — the start of season 3, around the year 707 (not fixed yet)
- **Turn length** — not decided yet; probably about one week of story time, or one major story beat
